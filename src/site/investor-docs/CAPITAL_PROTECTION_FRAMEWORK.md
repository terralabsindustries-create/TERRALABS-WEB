## 6. COMPARISON TO ALTERNATIVE STRUCTURES

### 6.1 Traditional Early-Stage Equity

**Structure:**
- 100% of capital deployed for operations
- Binary outcome: growth or loss

**Downside:**
- Total loss possible (60-70% of early-stage fail)

**Upside:**
- Potentially unlimited (if successful)

**Risk Profile:** High-risk, high-reward (venture capital model)

### 6.2 Revenue-Based Financing

**Structure:**
- Investor receives % of revenue until cap reached

**Downside:**
- If no revenue, no return

**Upside:**
- Capped return (typically 1.5-3x)

**Risk Profile:** Lower risk than equity, but still revenue-dependent

### 6.3 Convertible Note / SAFE

**Structure:**
- Debt instrument converting to equity at future round

**Downside:**
- If company fails before conversion, creditor in bankruptcy (often minimal recovery)

**Upside:**
- Discount + cap on conversion (typically 1.5-2.5x if successful)

**Risk Profile:** Moderate risk, moderate upside

### 6.4 TERRALABS Hybrid Structure

**Structure:**
- 40% capital protection mechanism (internal trading)
- 60% traditional equity deployment

**Downside:**
- Positive return even in zero-sales (1.4x demonstrated)

**Upside:**
- Full equity upside if business succeeds (8-20x potential)

**Risk Profile:** Asymmetric - engineered floor with unlimited upside

**Comparison Table:**
